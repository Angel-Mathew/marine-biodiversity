# Marine Biodiversity Dashboard

A dashboard that maps sightings of marine animals around the world and groups them by species group.

## The problem
Sightings data is just a long list of rows. I wanted to show where animals are seen and which groups are seen most often.

## What you can explore
- A world map of sightings, with details such as country and scientific name
- Sightings by species group
- Total sightings and number of unique species
- Two pages you can move between with the arrows

## What I found
Sea turtles, corals and seals are the most sighted groups, followed by pufferfish, penguins and sperm whales. The map shows sightings in many parts of the world, including Europe, Africa, Asia and the Americas.
